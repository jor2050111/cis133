HEADLINE (the page's one big heading)
---------------------------------------------------------------------

Copperwind Community

---------------------------------------------------------------------
WHAT THE PROGRAM IS (one paragraph, right under the headline)
---------------------------------------------------------------------

We are the community tech program of Copperwind IT Services, a
Phoenix IT company. The program collects unwanted phones, laptops,
cables, and chargers from around the Valley, and every donation
goes to a certified recycler. No experience required, no purchase
ever.

---------------------------------------------------------------------
WHAT WE DO (three short blocks, each with its own small heading)
---------------------------------------------------------------------

RECYCLING DRIVES
Twice a year the program takes over our parking lot and collects
everything the neighborhood no longer wants. The spring drive
brought in 154 items in one Saturday morning.

REPAIR CLINICS
Every donated device gets tested and wiped before the recycler
picks it up. Volunteers learn to do both at our clinics, and
nobody arrives knowing how.

DROP-IN HELP
We open the lobby weekly for tech questions, sorting donations,
and welcoming whoever walks in.
