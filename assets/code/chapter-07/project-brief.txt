=====================================================================
COPPERWIND COMMUNITY: SITE EXPANSION BRIEF
From Priya Sharma, outreach coordinator, Copperwind IT Services
Fall 2026
=====================================================================

You built us a three-page site, and it works. The recycling guide
answers the questions we used to answer one email at a time. The
gallery shows what a drive looks like. The contact page fills our
drop-off hours. Thank you.

Now the program is growing, and the site has to grow with it. This
brief tells you what we need. You decide how to build it. Show us
a plan before you build anything.

---------------------------------------------------------------------
WHAT THE SITE MUST ACCOMPLISH (THE PROGRAM'S GOALS)
---------------------------------------------------------------------

1. Keep electronics out of the landfill. More neighbors should find
   our drop-offs and drives, and arrive with devices ready to hand
   over.

2. Grow the volunteer crew. We want ten new volunteers by December,
   and the site should do the recruiting we cannot do in person.

3. Answer questions before they become emails. Drop-off hours, event
   dates, and what we accept should be findable without writing to
   the team.

---------------------------------------------------------------------
WHO VISITS THE SITE (TWO AUDIENCES)
---------------------------------------------------------------------

* Neighbors with a drawer full of electronics. They already know
  they want to recycle. They visit for one reason: when and where
  is the next event. They want that answer fast, from any page.

* Prospective volunteers. They arrive from a flyer, a friend, or a
  web search. They need to learn what the program does, see that we
  are welcoming, and find out how to join. If the first page they
  see confuses them, we lose them.

---------------------------------------------------------------------
WHAT WE NEED
---------------------------------------------------------------------

KEEP these three pages. They earn their place.

* The recycling guide (recycling-guide.html)
* The spring drive gallery (drive-gallery.html)
* The contact page (contact.html)

ADD three more.

1. A home page. The site has never had one. A first-time visitor
   lands on the recycling guide and has to guess what the program
   is. We want a front door: what the program is, what we do, and
   where to go next.

2. An events page. Visitors keep asking when the next drop-off is.
   Publish our fall schedule. Here is the raw content, straight
   from the outreach team's planning notes:

   * Fall Kickoff Drive. Saturday, September 12, 9:00 a.m. to
     noon, Copperwind HQ parking lot. Bring any item on the
     accepted list.
   * Repair Clinic. Saturday, September 26, 10:00 a.m. to
     1:00 p.m., Copperwind HQ training room. Watch a repair, or
     bring a device that needs one.
   * Device Drop-Off Day. Saturday, October 10, 9:00 a.m. to noon,
     Mesa Community Center. Drive up, drop off, done in minutes.
   * Digital Safety Workshop. Thursday, October 22, 6:00 to
     7:30 p.m., online. Passwords, backups, and safe browsing.
   * Volunteer Orientation. Saturday, November 7, 10:00 to
     11:30 a.m., Copperwind HQ training room. New volunteers
     welcome, no experience required.
   * Fall Wrap Drive. Saturday, November 21, 9:00 a.m. to noon,
     Copperwind HQ parking lot. Last drop-off day before the
     holidays.

3. A join page. We meet interested neighbors at every drive and
   then lose track of them. We want the site to collect the name
   and email of everyone who wants to hear from us, so a team
   member can invite them to the next event.

---------------------------------------------------------------------
WHAT WE ARE NOT ASKING FOR
---------------------------------------------------------------------

Do not redesign the program's look. The colors and the logo stay.
We like that every page already matches. Whatever you add should
match too.

Send your plan to copperwind-community@example.org. The outreach
team meets Tuesdays and will review it the week you send it.

=====================================================================
END OF BRIEF
