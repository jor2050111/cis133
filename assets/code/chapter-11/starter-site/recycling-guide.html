Recycle Your Old Electronics

Old phones, dead laptops, and tangled chargers pile up in drawers because nobody knows what to do with them. Copperwind Community collects unwanted electronics all year. Devices that can be saved are refurbished and donated, and the rest goes to a certified recycler.

This guide explains why electronics deserve better than the trash, how to prepare a device before you hand it over, and what the program accepts.

Why It Matters

Electronics contain metals worth recovering and materials that do not belong in a landfill. One recycled laptop keeps lead, mercury, and cadmium out of the ground and puts copper, aluminum, and even gold back into use.

Prepare Your Device: Three Steps

Complete these steps in order before you drop anything off.

  1. Back up anything you want to keep, such as photos, documents, and saved game data.
  2. Sign out of every account on the device, then perform a factory reset so none of your personal data travels with it.
  3. Remove any SIM card or memory card. The program recycles the device, not your accounts or your cards.

What the Program Accepts

Bring any item on this list to a collection event.

  • Phones and tablets
  • Computers and accessories
    • Laptops
    • Desktop towers
    • Keyboards and mice
  • Cables and chargers
  • Small electronics such as cameras and handheld games
