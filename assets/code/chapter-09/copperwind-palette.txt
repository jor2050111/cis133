CIS133 SKILLS LAB 4A: THE COPPERWIND DESIGN SET PALETTE
=====================================================================

Every color below comes straight from the Copperwind illustration set,
the recorded design set behind the logo, the charts, and the drive scenes.
The hex values are exact. Copy them from this file into your CSS
instead of retyping them from memory.

The labels in [BRACKETS] name each color. They are notes to you.
They never go into your stylesheet.

---------------------------------------------------------------------

[COPPERWIND TEAL]
#268080
The Copperwind brand color: the logo's ring, the drive canopy,
and the collection bins.
A strong region background behind white text.

[DEEP TEAL]
#1a5e5e
The darker teal of the mountain silhouettes. A heading color on light
backgrounds, or a background behind white text.

[SUNSET ORANGE]
#f4a259
The logo's wind ribbon and the warm sky bands. A warm accent
background behind
dark text.

[LIGHT SAND]
#fac78d
The set's palest tone, from the sunlit sand. A page background that
keeps dark text readable.

[SAND]
#deb887
The desert ground under every scene. A calm region background
behind dark text.

[SAGUARO GREEN]
#5e9959
The saguaro green, with a sorting bin to match. An accent for
small touches. Too light to
sit behind white body text.

[INK]
#333333
The dark gray of the chart text and darkest outlines. The site's body
text color.

[WHITE]
#ffffff
The figure plates and the set's highlights. A page background, or the
text color on either teal.

TWO LOOKALIKES TO LEAVE ALONE

The set also mixes two browns for background props. Both sit close
to SAND, and neither earned a job on the site. Choosing SAND over
these two is a design decision, not a coin flip.
