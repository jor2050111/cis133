The Winter Drive Is Coming

Copperwind Community's winter recycling drive lands Saturday, February 6, at the HQ parking lot collection table. Volunteers will take phones, laptops, cables, and chargers from nine until two, and every device moves on the same week, to a new home or a certified recycler.

Prepare your device before you arrive. Back up what you want to keep, sign out of every account, and perform a factory reset. The program recycles the hardware, never your data, and the table's volunteers can walk you through any step you have not done.
